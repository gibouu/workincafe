# Decision records

Authority order:

1. Current technical-lead instruction.
2. Ratified operative decision records under `docs/decisions/source/`.
3. The condensed register (`register.md`) and deferred register
   (`deferred-register.md`).
4. `AGENTS.md` and supporting governance documents.

If a summary conflicts with its operative record, the operative record
controls until explicitly amended by a later recorded decision. The
operative records are normalized and reorganized from the ratified July
2026 decision process; they preserve every operative requirement,
amendment, supersession and trigger, and are authoritative because the
technical lead ratified them.

Private compliance evidence remains private. These records reference its
existence without exposing confidential correspondence; location
references are added only after verification. Statements about external
account state are marked `Target requirement` until verified.

New decisions: propose → technical-lead approval → add or amend an
operative record in `source/` → update `register.md` (and
`deferred-register.md` where dispositions change). Never edit a ruling
silently; amendments append with date. Superseded portions are marked
SUPERSEDED, never deleted.
