# Paris shortlist — proposed seed list (not curated)

Status: **proposal for the operator review queue.** Nothing here is a
candidate, a publication or a curated value. Under Decision 31 (once ratified)
each venue still goes through the normal seeding and human-review flow; this
file only records where the ideas came from.

Compiled 2026-11-15/19 by the product owner (Souad) from two sorting passes:

- **Social shortlist:** cafés people share for laptop work on public TikTok
  videos, plus the top picks of laptop-friendly guides published in 2026.
- **OpenStreetMap pass:** Paris cafés from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap
  contributors, ODbL), filtered to likely coffee shops.

No Google content (ratings, reviews, photos, hours) is stored here — names,
street addresses and public source links only. Coordinates come from
OpenStreetMap / Nominatim and are approximate.
